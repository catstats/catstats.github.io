I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Purdue University in 2024 and my master’s degree in Statistics from Purdue University in 2019. I earned my bachelor’s degree in Statistics and Operations Research from Hong Kong Baptist University in 2017. I worked as a full-stack data scientist at Sysco.

My research interests include representation learning, explainable artificial intelligence, causal inference, and bioinformatics. I also develop open-source software for statistical computing and reproducible research.
